Issue links: [#3330874](https://www.drupal.org/node/3330874), [#3413268](https://www.drupal.org/node/3413268)

Drupal 11.0 will require PHP 8.3.

PHP 8.3 has been supported in Drupal 10 since the 10.2.0 release, and Drupal 10 installs recommend at least PHP 8.2 or higher.
