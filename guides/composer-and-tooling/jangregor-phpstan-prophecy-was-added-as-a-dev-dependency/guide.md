`jangregor/phpstan-prophecy` ([https://github.com/Jan0707/phpstan-prophecy](https://github.com/Jan0707/phpstan-prophecy)) was added as a dev-dependency.

This package allows resolving Prophecy mocks into their mocked types, and avoids false positives in the PHPStan baseline.
