Introduced in branch: 12.0.x\
Introduced in version: 12.0.0\
Issue links: [#3526560](https://www.drupal.org/node/3526560)

The Umami demonstration profile in core is not demonstrating the full power of Drupal anymore. Drupal CMS now offers a dozen site templates which are created for a variety of use cases and themed in a variety of visual styles. Site templates also rely on contributed projects on top of serving relevant sample content.

While hidden from users, the Umami demonstration profile is still used for performance testing Drupal core, so it is kept in the codebase for now.

The Umami profile contains recipes with photos that an enthusiastic team of folks cooked and baked and photographed. It would be a worthy future for the profile to be converted to a contributed site template on top of Drupal CMS. See [#3523324: Deprecate and remove the Umami demonstration profile from core, make it a contributed site template](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupal/issues/3523324).
