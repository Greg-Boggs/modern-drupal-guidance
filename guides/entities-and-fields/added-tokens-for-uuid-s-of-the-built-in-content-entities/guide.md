Introduced in branch: 11.1.x\
Introduced in version: 11.1.0\
Issue links: [#3466573](https://www.drupal.org/node/3466573)

The following new tokens were introduced for accessing UUID on Drupal core's built-in content entities:

- `[comment:uuid]`
- `[file:uuid]`
- `[node:uuid`\]
- `[taxonomy:uuid]`
- `[user:uuid]`
