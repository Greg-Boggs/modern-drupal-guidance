## Goal

Create a Vendor role to allow some - but not all - users to perform specific
tasks.
## Prerequisite knowledge

{{ GUIDE_REF("user-concept") }}
## Steps

1. In the _Manage_ administrative menu, navigate to _People_ > _Roles_
    (_admin/people/roles_).
1. You will find default roles _Anonymous user_,
    _Authenticated user_, and _Administrator_ already present.
    ![Roles page](https://www.drupal.org/docs/user_guide/en/images/user-new-role-roles-page.png)
1. Click _Add Role_ to add a custom role.
1. Type Vendor in the _Role name_ field. Click _Save_.
    ![adding vendor as a role](https://www.drupal.org/docs/user_guide/en/images/user-new-role-add-role.png)
1. You will see the message "Role Vendor has been added." displayed at the top
    of the page.
    ![vendor role added success message](https://www.drupal.org/docs/user_guide/en/images/user-new-role-confirm.png)
