Introduced in branch: 11.5.x\
Introduced in version: 11.5.0\
Issue links: [#3611847](https://www.drupal.org/node/3611847)

The following `PdoTraits` methods and the `$fetchModeLiterals` property are deprecated. There are no replacements.

- `pdoToFetchAs()`
- `clientFetchColumn()`
- `clientFetchObject()`
- `clientQueryString()`

*Source: [pdoToFetchAs, clientFetchColumn, clientFetchObject, clientQueryString, $fetchModeLiterals from PdoTrait are deprecated](https://www.drupal.org/node/3611848), a Drupal core change record by [mondrake](https://www.drupal.org/user/1307444) on drupal.org, licensed under [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).*
