Introduced in branch: 11.1.x\
Introduced in version: 11.1.0\
Issue links: [#3421202](https://www.drupal.org/node/3421202)

The `\Drupal\FunctionalJavascriptTests\WebDriverCurlService` is deprecated. Any calls to `\Drupal\FunctionalJavascriptTests\WebDriverCurlService::enableRetry()` and \Drupal\FunctionalJavascriptTests\WebDriverCurlService::disableRetry() will trigger deprecation messages. The class is no longer registered in `\Drupal\FunctionalJavascriptTests\DrupalSelenium2Driver`.
