Introduced in version: 11.1.0\
Issue links: [#3466788](https://www.drupal.org/node/3466788), [#3468204](https://www.drupal.org/node/3468204)

'Risky' tests are now configured to let the PHPUnit runner fail.

Risky tests are those that perform no assertions or have no expectations set.
