Introduced in branch: 11.2.x\
Introduced in version: 11.2.0\
Issue links: [#3522505](https://www.drupal.org/node/3522505)

Calling \Drupal\Core\Extension\ThemeInstaller::\__construct() without the $componentPluginManager argument is deprecated in drupal:11.2.0 and it will be required in drupal:12.0.0.

*Source: [Calling \Drupal\Core\Extension\ThemeInstaller::\__construct() without the $componentPluginManager argument is deprecated](https://www.drupal.org/node/3525649), a Drupal core change record by [acbramley](https://www.drupal.org/user/1036766) on drupal.org, licensed under [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).*
