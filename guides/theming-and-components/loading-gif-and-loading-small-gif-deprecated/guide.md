Introduced in branch: 11.1.x\
Introduced in version: 11.1.0\
Issue links: [#2575253](https://www.drupal.org/node/2575253)

The animated GIFs `core/misc/loading.gif` and `core/misc/loading-small.gif` icons look poor on HiDPI displays so they have been deprecated to be removed in Drupal 12.

Themes should use `core/misc/loading.svg` and `core/misc/loading-small.svg` instead.
