Introduced in version: 12.0.0\
Issue links: [#3586221](https://www.drupal.org/node/3586221)

The Stable 9 theme is removed.

The Stable 9 theme was deprecated in Drupal 11.4.0 and the theme moved to contrib at [https://drupal.org/project/stable9](https://drupal.org/project/stable9)

If you need the functionality provided by the Stable 9 theme, read the [recommendations for the Stable 9 theme](https://www.drupal.org/node/3223395#s-stable9).
